Why a Graph Database?

Alumni networks are fundamentally about relationships: alumni mentor other alumni, share skills, and work at connected companies. A graph database models these connections directly, making queries such as “find alumni with Python skills” or “find mentors within two hops” simple, efficient, and easy to explain compared with multiple relational joins.
